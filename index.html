Draw over anything

Pen, highlighter, line, arrow, box, circle and text in your colours or a gradient. Ink can erase itself after a few seconds, or switch to a whiteboard.

Cursor highlight

A halo that follows your pointer: circle, ring, squircle or rhombus, any colour, border and glow. It can fade when you stop moving.

Spotlight and zoom

Dim everything except what you're pointing at, or open a zoom lens that follows the cursor so tiny text reads on a projector.

Your shortcuts, your look

Every action has a global shortcut you can change, plus a menu bar menu. Colours, sizes and styles are all adjustable in Settings.

Shortcuts that work in every app

Defaults. Change any of them in the app's Settings.

While you're drawing

The toolbar has every tool; these keys are faster. Press / to see them on screen.
