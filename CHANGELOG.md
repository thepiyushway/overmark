# Changelog

Each `## <version>` section becomes the notes of that GitHub Release. People see them under "What's new…" when Overmark offers the update.

## 1.0.0-beta.2
New tools, after a feature comparison with Epic Pen:

- **Eraser** (E): rub out just part of your ink, in three sizes (16, 36 and 80 px). A ring at the pointer shows exactly what will be erased. It works on pen, highlighter and text, never cuts the whiteboard, and can be undone.
- **Screenshot** (C, the camera button, the menu bar, or your own shortcut): saves the screen with your ink to the Desktop and copies it to the clipboard. The toolbar is hidden while it captures, and quick repeats never overwrite each other.
- **Custom colour**: the eighth swatch opens 24 quick colours; key 8 selects it; any shade in Settings → Annotate.
- **Each tool keeps its own size**: a thick pen, a thin highlighter and a big eraser at the same time.
- **Pen pressure**: with a stylus or drawing tablet, pressing harder draws thicker lines.
- Auto-erase (fading ink) moves from E to **F**, because E is now the eraser.

## 1.0.0-beta.1
First public release of the downloadable Mac app.

- Draw over any app: pen, highlighter, line, arrow, box, circle and text. Six colours plus gradient ink; Shift for straight angles, perfect squares and circles; undo and redo. Undo also brings back ink you cleared by mistake.
- Whiteboard (white, dotted or dark) with its own ink.
- Cursor highlight, spotlight and a zoom lens, all adjustable, with live previews in Settings.
- Global shortcuts you can change; a menu bar menu with every action; auto-erase; annotate without the toolbar.
- Works without Claude and offline. First launch opens Settings; afterwards Overmark lives in the menu bar.
- Updates itself: you're told when a new version is out, in a notification, in Settings and on screen, and it installs from the menu bar after checking the download.
